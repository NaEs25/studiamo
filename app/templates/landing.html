Why I Built Studiamo

K

Karl

Creator & Founder of Studiamo

2 min read

It was two weeks before my final exams. I was sitting in the library, surrounded by macroeconomics flashcards. My calendar was packed. Suddenly, my phone lit up. A message from my favorite client: "Hey Karl, we have a massive new project here. Can you edit a video like this for us?"

I opened the reference and froze. The video looked incredible, but it was on a level I had never edited before. My first thought: "I can easily pull this off if I just watch a few good tutorials."

But when I opened YouTube, reality set in: I had actually watched a tutorial on this exact topic a while ago, and I completely forgot it. Back then, I felt productive, and now when I actually needed the knowledge, it was gone. Just watching without active recall meant nothing stuck.

That same evening, I was browsing YouTube again to unwind. A video was recommended to me titled "The Last Spice Video You’ll Ever Need." I really wanted to learn it (If I learn this now, I'll know it for the rest of my life). But an inner voice told me: I'll watch it, feel productive for a moment, and then forget everything again.

So I added it to my Watch Later list. The list opened up and overwhelmed me with 53 videos I had once planned to watch but had long since forgotten. I realized I was shutting down mentally. I stopped letting any new knowledge in, out of fear of forgetting what mattered most.

I somehow had to grind through those two weeks to get through both the client video and my exams at the same time.

Only after I passed my exams and delivered the finished video did I realize: I needed a system that locks in my knowledge permanently without overloading my mind.

That’s when the idea for the app was born. A place where educational videos don't get lost in the feed, but are saved, structured by goals. Featuring short, automated quizzes right after watching to anchor the material straight into long-term memory.

During development, there were moments when everything broke. When I split the code into smaller files for better organization, the entire codebase collapsed and I ran out of AI tokens. I sat there in the middle of the night staring at the ruins of my program, having to fight my way back line by line from a backup.

But it was worth it.

Today, I open my app, learn with focus without a distracting feed, and stay in control.

Passive video watching is entertainment. Active recall is learning.

Während der Entwicklung gab es Momente, in denen gar nichts mehr ging. Als mir beim Aufteilen des Codes in mehrere kleine Dateien der Code komplett zerschoss und die KI-Tokens ausgingen, saß ich nachts vor den Trümmern meines Programms und musste mich Zeile für Zeile aus einem Backup zurückkämpfen.

Aber es hat sich gelohnt.

Heute öffne ich meine App, lerne gezielt ohne Ablenkungs-Feed und behalte die Kontrolle.

Passives Videoschauen ist Unterhaltung. Aktive Wiederholung ist Lernen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on algorithms, privacy, open source, and how Studiamo transforms your study routine.

What makes Studiamo different from Anki?

Anki is designed for manual, high-density deck craft with complex configurations. Studiamo is built for zero friction: paste any YouTube lecture link or upload a PDF, and Studiamo instantly generates structured summaries, fact-checks claims against the transcript, and creates interactive active recall quizzes with zero manual card writing.

How does Studiamo prevent AI hallucinations in quizzes?

Studiamo extracts verbatim transcripts and document text directly from the source. It uses Google Gemini models instructed to strictly ground recall questions and conceptual explanations in verified transcript quotes, cross-checking key claims before presenting flashcards.

Is Studiamo free and open source?

Yes. Studiamo is AGPL-3.0 licensed and 100% free to self-host with Docker Compose using your own Google Gemini API key. There is also a managed cloud subscription ($7.99/month) for users who want a hassle-free, ready-to-use experience without hosting infrastructure.

What is the science behind testing myself instead of re-watching?

In landmark research published in Science, Karpicke and Blunt (2011) showed that retrieval practice produces about a 50% improvement in long-term retention over passive re-reading or elaborate note-taking. The researchers found that "retrieval is not merely a read-out of knowledge; the act of reconstructing knowledge itself enhances learning." Even attempting recall and missing an answer primes your memory to encode the correct explanation when revealed. Studiamo applies this by removing the friction of manual card writing, automatically turning your educational videos into instant self-quizzes.

How do study reminders and notifications work?

Because Studiamo is a web application, it supports optional notification channels via Telegram, browser web push, and email. When spaced repetition reviews are due, you receive an alert with a link to open your study queue directly in your browser.

What cognitive science research backs Studiamo?

Studiamo is grounded in cognitive psychology research on practice testing (Dunlosky et al., 2013; Roediger & Karpicke, 2006; Karpicke & Blunt, 2011). These empirical studies prove that active retrieval practice outperforms passive consumption across subjects, breaking the common "illusion of competence" that comes from video watching.
