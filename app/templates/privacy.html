Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026 • Domain: studiamo.cloud

1. Overview

Studiamo ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you visit or use studiamo.cloud and our active recall spaced repetition application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in via Google Single Sign-On (SSO), we collect your verified email address, display name, and unique authentication identifier.
  • Study & Learning Progress Data: Active recall quiz attempts, Spaced Repetition System (SRS) intervals, review histories, study streaks, and earned experience points (XP).
  • User Content: Study materials, notes, or YouTube URLs submitted for active recall question generation.
  • Waitlist Information: When you join our pre-launch waitlist, we collect your email address, hosting preference, referring page, approximate country (from your network provider), and browser user agent.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Deliver personalized active recall quizzes and calculate optimal Spaced Repetition review timing.
  • Display optional leaderboard rankings (which can be toggled to anonymous at any time in Profile settings).
  • Process AI flashcard generation through secure Google Gemini API interfaces.

4. Third-Party Services & Sub-Processors

Google OAuth & AI Studio

Used for secure Single Sign-On authentication and Gemini AI quiz question generation.

YouTube API Services

Used to fetch video metadata, thumbnails, and study video recommendations.

Umami Analytics

Privacy-focused, cookie-free web analytics respecting user anonymity.

Supabase

Database hosting (EU, Frankfurt) for account and study data on the cloud version.

Lemon Squeezy

Merchant of Record for subscriptions (Sold through Link, LLC, USA). Handles checkout, payment and invoicing. Receives your email address and billing details directly. We never see or store your card data.

Resend

Transactional and waitlist email delivery.

Cloudflare

DNS, TLS termination and reverse proxy for studiamo.cloud (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Every request reaches us through it, so it processes your IP address and request metadata to route traffic and block abuse.

YouTube API Services & Google Data: Studiamo uses YouTube API Services to retrieve public video metadata (video titles, creator/channel names, duration, and thumbnail images), search educational video suggestions, and render in-app video study players. By using these features, you acknowledge that public YouTube data is accessed in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or revoke access to your Google account permissions at any time via the Google Security Settings page.

Subscription data we store consists only of your subscription status, renewal and end dates, and the identifiers Lemon Squeezy uses for your customer and subscription records. No payment card details are transmitted to or held by us at any point.

5. Cookies We Use

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary to provide a service you've explicitly requested (like signing in), never for advertising, cross-site tracking, or profiling. Because of that, these don't require a cookie-consent banner under applicable law (TTDSG §25(2) / ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3)), but we're listing them here for transparency:

yb_session

Keeps you signed in. HttpOnly (not readable by JavaScript). Expires when you log out or after a long period of inactivity.

username

Lets the interface show which account is active. Works alongside the session cookie above.

ref_code

Temporary (24 hours). Holds a referral code so a friend's invite is credited if you sign up after clicking their link. Contains no personal data.

orig_ref

Temporary (24 hours). Preserves the initial external site or campaign that referred you so signup attribution remains accurate. Contains no personal data.

6. Your Rights & Data Control

Under applicable privacy regulations (including GDPR), you have the right to access, export, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. You can toggle anonymous leaderboard visibility or modify profile preferences in your account Settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data protection rights, contact us at: hello@studiamo.cloud.
