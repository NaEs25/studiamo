H1: Your Watch Later list is not a to-do list

Most people's Watch Later list is a graveyard. Videos go in faster than they come out, and
the longer it gets, the less likely you are to open it. The list stops being a plan and
becomes a reminder of what you have not done.

## Why the list keeps growing

Saving a video feels like a small win: you recognized something worth learning. But saving
costs nothing and watching costs time, so the list only goes one way.

## A way to shrink it

1. **Delete without guilt.** If you have not wanted a video in three months, you will not
   want it in six. Remove it.
2. **Group by goal.** Keep only videos that serve something you are working on now.
3. **Watch with a question.** Before you press play, write what you want to know when it ends.
4. **Quiz yourself right after.** Two minutes of recall at the end changes what you keep.
5. **Review a day later and a week later.** Short, scheduled, from memory.

## Watching is not the end

The video is the start of learning, not the finish. A watched video you cannot remember is
worth about as much as an unwatched one, and costs more of your day.
